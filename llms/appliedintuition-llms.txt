# Applied Intuition

> Physical AI that Moves the World.

Applied Intuition builds physical AI software and systems for intelligent machines. The company helps customers develop, test, validate, deploy, and operate autonomy and vehicle intelligence across automotive, defense, trucking, mining, construction, and agriculture. Core areas include self-driving systems, vehicle operating systems, simulation, validation, data workflows, and frontier research in autonomy and robotics.
